# 2D crystals beyond in-plane modes: out-of-plane dipoles, environment and radiative widths

Labels: **[est]** established literature; **[derived]** worked out here from EPP's equations, not printed in EPP; **[proposal]** suggested extension; **[spec]** speculation. *Illustrative* numbers use the primer's hBN preset ($S_{\rm uc}=5.43$ Å², $\mu=6.10$ u, $Z^*_\parallel=2.7$, $\hbar\omega_{\rm T}=170$ meV, $r_0=10$ Å). Out-of-plane numbers use a **placeholder** $Z^*_{zz}=0.5$ and $\hbar\omega_{\rm ZO}=0.1$ eV, to be replaced by computed values (§4(d)).

## 1. What is missing in EPP

EPP §8 (E.455–E.472, pp. 100–102; primer [§B.10](../../sections/B10-2d.html)) treats one **planar** sheet. The ionic bubble is pinned to $z=0$ (E.455), the photon line obeys a $z$-resolved Dyson equation (p. 100, unnumbered), and then three restrictions are made at once:

1. **Out-of-plane dipoles are dropped.** E.300 and E.305 (pp. 64, 67) contain all $\alpha,\beta\in\{x,y,z\}$, hence $Z^*_{zz}$. Before E.463 the "divergent local term $\sim\delta(z-z')$" is discarded and only $\alpha,\beta=x,y$ are kept, so ZO-type modes (hBN ZO, MoS₂ A₂″) get no self-energy.
2. **Atomic heights are lost.** The printed E.300/E.305 put every dipole at $z=0$; the corrected E.300′/E.305′ ([§Z.3](../../sections/Z03-corrections.html)) carry $\boldsymbol\rho^0_a$ and $z_a$, but §8 is evaluated only for $z_a=0$. Multi-plane sheets (MoS₂, InSe), multilayers and heterostructures are absent.
3. **Strictly 2D, electrostatic, freestanding.** The bubble is squeezed into $\delta(z)$ (E p.101′; RPA, no local fields, no $f_{xc}$), $c\to\infty$ (E.460), and there is no substrate (dielectric or metallic) or finite thickness. The primer's $\epsilon_{\rm env}+r_0Q$ is a literature parameterization, not EPP.

Also missing: the finite-$c$ sheet propagator (the primer derives only its in-plane block), radiative widths, 2D phonon polaritons, and any evaluated 2D electron–phonon vertex. EPP p. 86 (after E.385) asserts that "the transition to 2D case is trivial": with $\Omega\to S$, $\Omega_{\rm uc}\to S_{\rm uc}$, $\mathbf q\to\mathbf Q$, $\mathbf k\to\mathbf K$ "formally all expressions are identical". But E.415–E.416 are never evaluated with the sheet propagator, and that replacement fails for the photon line, whose 2D kernel $v_Q=2\pi/Q$ comes from the $q_z$ integral (§B.10). So there is no 2D Fröhlich coupling, mobility or polaron in EPP ([§Z.1](../../sections/Z01-outlook.html)).

## 2. Why it matters

- **LO dispersion and environment [est].** LO and TO are degenerate at Γ. The LO slope scales with $Z^{*2}$, is reduced by a dielectric environment, and grows linearly with layer number (Sohier *et al.* 2017). For illustrative hBN the freestanding slope is 0.245 eV Å. For a hypothetical non-dispersive, nonpolar environment with $\epsilon_{\rm env}=4.5$ it would fall to 0.054 eV Å ($1/\epsilon_{\rm env}$ scaling; primer). This is a scaling illustration only: 4.5 is the static hBN-encapsulation value, but at 170 meV an hBN environment is inside its own Reststrahlen band, and hBN-in-hBN is bulk (see the polar-substrate pitfall, §5). EELS of monolayer hBN on Cu foil, a **metallic** substrate, finds an LO slope below the freestanding value, attributed to substrate screening (Li *et al.* 2024). A metal does not act as a dielectric $\epsilon_{\rm env}$. Its image charges make the LO shift quadratic in $Q$ for $Qd\ll1$ (§4(a)), so the Cu data can only be compared with a metallic-substrate kernel.
- **Out-of-plane dipoles in transport [est].** Omitting the 2D dipolar correction (which includes out-of-plane dipoles) overestimates the 300 K hole mobility of fluorinated graphene, (C₂F₂)ₙ, by 99%, and a 3D correction underestimates it by 33% (Deng *et al.* 2021; numbers from arXiv:2010.02437). Neither number isolates the out-of-plane dipoles. Quadrupoles can change computed 2D mobilities by more than 75% in some of the materials studied (Poncé *et al.* 2023). 2D Fröhlich coupling in TMDs is much stronger than earlier ab initio estimates (Sohier *et al.* 2016).
- **ZO dispersion [est] (Royo & Stengel 2021); re-derived here from EPP's propagator.** Royo and Stengel identified a previously overlooked dipolar term involving the out-of-plane components of the dynamical charges, which gives ZO branches a linear dispersion near Γ (tested on monolayer BN, SnS₂ and BaTiO₃ membranes). In EPP's language, the dropped $zz$ term is not only a $Q$-independent contact shift: its nonlocal part *softens* ZO modes linearly in $Q$ (§4(d)). This corrects the statement in [§B.10](../../sections/B10-2d.html) that ZO-type modes "receive a $Q$-independent depolarization shift instead". The contact term is $Q$-independent, but the nonlocal part gives a $Q$-linear softening; §B.10 should be updated. With the placeholder $Z^*_{zz}=0.5$ (open-circuit 2D charge, §4(d)) and $\hbar\omega_{\rm ZO}=0.1$ eV, the slope is $-0.014$ eV Å, about $-1.4$ meV at $Q=0.1$ Å⁻¹.
- **Radiative widths [derived].** In-plane polar modes of freestanding hBN have $\hbar\gamma_0\approx42$ μeV at $Q=0$ (primer, finite $c$). ZO modes are dark at normal incidence, with width $\propto x^2$ (§4(e)). The same angular laws ($\cos\theta$, $1/\cos\theta$, $\sin^2\theta/\cos\theta$) govern 2D excitons. Bright in-plane excitons have far larger, meV-scale radiative widths; $z$-polarized dark excitons are much weaker and visible only off normal incidence (Wang *et al.* 2018).
- **For an RPA/GW/BSE practitioner.** The phonon kernel *is* your $W$: $r_0$, $\epsilon_{\rm env}$ and Coulomb truncation enter the LO dispersion exactly as they enter Rytova–Keldysh exciton binding. Without truncation a supercell restores a spurious LO–TO splitting (Sohier *et al.* 2017), the artifact that also spoils untruncated 2D BSE. A wrong $r_0$ convention (§5) moves the LO plateau $\sqrt{\omega_{\rm T}^2+C'/r_0}$.

## 3. What standard theory does

Giustino's review is three-dimensional: the Verdi–Giustino vertex (G.180) with $\bq\cdot\epsilon^\infty\cdot\bq$, the Gonze–Lee term (cited, not printed), $\Pi^{\rm A}$ (G.125) and $W_{\rm e}=v+vP_{\rm e}W_{\rm e}$ (G.94) ([§A.4](../../sections/A04-polar.html), [§A.6](../../sections/A06-phonon-se.html)).

Established 2D first-principles practice [est]:

- **DFPT with a Coulomb cutoff along $z$** removes periodic images and handles gating (Sohier, Calandra, Mauri 2016, 2017).
- **Long-range force constants.** The 2D dipole term (Sohier *et al.* 2017) was made exact by Royo and Stengel (2021): 2×2 (in-plane/out-of-plane) screening, $z$-dependent macroscopic potentials, the out-of-plane dynamical-charge term behind the linear ZO dispersion, quadrupoles. They also discuss how the 2D out-of-plane charge relates to the bulk-like supercell one.
- **Long-range e–ph vertices.** 2D dipoles with out-of-plane charges (Deng *et al.* 2021); a unified 3D↔2D Fröhlich vertex without truncation (Sio and Giustino 2022); dipoles, quadrupoles and a Berry-connection term for mobilities of MoS₂, InSe, hBN (Poncé *et al.* 2023).
- **Environment.** Layer-resolved screening (QEH, Andersen *et al.* 2015), remote screening of Fröhlich scattering (Sohier, Gibertini, Verstraete 2021), $r_0$ from the sheet polarizability (Cudazzo *et al.* 2011; Berkelbach *et al.* 2013).
- **Electrodynamics and excitons.** The 2D phonon polariton is the LO phonon (Rivera, Christensen, Narang 2019). Phonon screening of the BSE kernel was formulated for 3D, isotropic halide perovskites (CsPbX₃) with a 3D hydrogenic model (Filip, Haber, Neaton 2021); its 2D generalization would use the sheet LO dispersion of [§B.10](../../sections/B10-2d.html).

## 4. How it could enter the EPP formalism

**(a) Full sheet propagator [derived from E.375].** Write $\tilde\partial=(iQ_x,iQ_y,\partial_z)$, $\tilde\partial'=(-iQ_x,-iQ_y,\partial_{z'})$, $k^2=\epsilon\omega^2/c^2$ and $\beta=\sqrt{Q^2-k^2-i\eta}$ with ${\rm Re}\,\beta>0$. Then, in a homogeneous medium $\epsilon$,
$$D^0_{\mu\nu}(\mathbf Q,\omega;z,z')=\frac{4\pi c}{\epsilon\,\omega^2}\Big[k^2\delta_{\mu\nu}-\tilde\partial_\mu\tilde\partial'_\nu\Big]\frac{e^{-\beta|z-z'|}}{2\beta}.$$
In-plane it gives $\frac{2\pi c}{\epsilon\omega^2\beta}(k^2\delta_{\mu\nu}-Q_\mu Q_\nu)$ (the primer's result); $xz\propto-iQ_x\,{\rm sgn}(z-z')\,e^{-\beta|z-z'|}$, nonzero only between different heights; and $zz=\frac{4\pi c}{\epsilon\omega^2}\big[\frac{Q^2}{2\beta}e^{-\beta|z-z'|}-\delta(z-z')\big]$, whose $\delta$ is EPP's "divergent local term". For $c\to\infty$ it reduces to E.460 with $W\to v_Qe^{-Q|z-z'|}$.

*Layered environment* **[proposal].** In a stack, the tensor form above with only the scalar factor replaced does not survive: TE (s) and TM (p) waves reflect with different coefficients, and $\epsilon$ depends on $z$. Replace $D^0$ by the dyadic Green's function of the stack, decomposed into s and p parts with $r_s$ and $r_p$. Its electrostatic limit is the image kernel. For a sheet in medium a at height $d$ above a substrate b,
$$W(z,z')=\frac{v_Q}{\epsilon_a}\Big[e^{-Q|z-z'|}+\frac{\epsilon_a-\epsilon_b}{\epsilon_a+\epsilon_b}\,e^{-Q(z+z'+2d)}\Big].$$

*Metallic substrate* **[derived, illustrative]**. A perfect conductor ($\epsilon_b\to\infty$) gives $v_Q[e^{-Q|z-z'|}-e^{-Q(z+z'+2d)}]$. In-plane dipoles have antiparallel images: $W(0,0)=v_Q(1-e^{-2Qd})\to4\pi d$ for $Qd\ll1$, so the LO shift becomes quadratic, $\propto4\pi dQ^2$, not a reduced linear slope. Out-of-plane dipoles have parallel images: the ZO $Q$-linear term becomes $-\frac{2\pi Q}{S_{\rm uc}}(1+e^{-2Qd})|j_{z\sigma}|^2$, nearly doubled. With the hBN preset (sheet screening $r_0$ kept), $d=3$ Å and $Q=0.05$ Å⁻¹, the LO shift drops from 8.0 to 2.8 meV, while the ZO shift (placeholder $Z^*_{zz}$, in-plane screening of the image field neglected) grows from $-0.72$ to $-1.25$ meV. A realistic Cu response (Drude/Thomas–Fermi, or Lindhard) replaces $-1$ by $r_p(Q,\omega)$, with the hBN–Cu distance $d$ as an input.

**(b) Ionic sheet self-energy [proposal].** Keep E.300′/E.305′ with all three Cartesian components and every height $z_a$, in the subtracted form (the analogue of E.311):
$$\Pi^{\rm ion}_{jj,\alpha\beta}(\mathbf Q,\omega;z,z')=-\frac{\omega^2}{cS_{\rm uc}}\sum_{\sigma=1}^{3s}\sum_{ab}\frac{j^a_{\alpha\sigma}(\mathbf Q)\,j^{b*}_{\beta\sigma}(\mathbf Q)}{\omega^2-\omega_\sigma^2(\mathbf Q)}\,\delta(z-z_a)\,\delta(z'-z_b).$$

**(c) Phonon self-energy [proposal, generalizes E.458].** With $J^a_{\alpha\sigma}=\sqrt{\hbar/(2cS_{\rm uc}\omega_\sigma)}\,\omega\,j^a_{\alpha\sigma}$:
$$\Omega^{\rm e}_{\sigma\sigma'}(\mathbf Q,\omega)=-\sum_{ab}\sum_{\alpha\beta\in\{x,y,z\}}J^{a*}_{\alpha\sigma}\,D^{\rm e}_{\alpha\beta}(\mathbf Q,\omega;z_a,z_b)\,J^b_{\beta\sigma'},$$
with $D^{\rm e}$ from the $z$-resolved Dyson equation of p. 100 built on (a). E.467 holds in the diagonal approximation. In sheets without $\sigma_h$ and with $z_a\neq z_b$ (Janus, buckled), $K_{xz}(z_a,z_b)\neq0$ couples LO-like and ZO-like modes, so $\Omega^{\rm e}_{\sigma\sigma'}$ has off-diagonal elements. Then solve the matrix Dyson equation E.398 (or a dynamical-matrix form analogous to E.454a, [§B.9](../../sections/B09-electrostatic.html)).

**(d) Electrostatic kernel [derived].** As $c\to\infty$ the photon line becomes $-\frac{c}{\omega^2}K$, with $K_{\alpha\beta}(z,z')=\tilde\partial_\alpha\tilde\partial'_\beta W^{\rm e}(\mathbf Q,\omega;z,z')$. For the bare kernel,
$$\partial_z\partial_{z'}\,v_Qe^{-Q|z-z'|}=4\pi\,\delta(z-z')-Q^2v_Qe^{-Q|z-z'|}.$$
For the bare, translation-invariant kernel, $K_{xx}+K_{yy}+K_{zz}=4\pi\delta(z-z')$ (Laplace): off the contact, what the in-plane longitudinal mode gains ($+2\pi Q$), the $z$ mode loses ($-2\pi Q$). The screened $K$ is not traceless. The induced term of E.461, $v_Q^2\Pi\,e^{-Q(|z|+|z'|)}$, gives $\partial_z\partial_{z'}\to+Q^2{\rm sgn}(z){\rm sgn}(z')W_{\rm ind}$, so the trace is $2Q^2W_{\rm ind}$ for $z,z'$ on the same side; the LO gets $2\pi Q/\epsilon_{\rm 2D}$ while the ZO keeps $-2\pi Q$. For a planar, $\sigma_h$-symmetric sheet the in-plane modes recover E.472 and the ZO modes decouple, with, to leading order in $Q$,
$$\omega^2_{\rm ZO}(\mathbf Q)=\bar\omega^2_{\rm ZO}-\frac{2\pi Q}{S_{\rm uc}}\,|j_{z\sigma}|^2 ,$$
where $\bar\omega_{\rm ZO}$ contains the contact (depolarization) shift. This is the linear ZO dispersion of Royo and Stengel (2021), re-derived from EPP's propagator. The in-plane sheet bubble cannot screen the $Q$-linear term, since a $z$-dipole sheet has $\phi(z=0)=0$. Here $Z^*_{zz}$ (inside $j_{z\sigma}$) is the **open-circuit (D-field) 2D charge**, which the vacuum kernel requires because it has no $\alpha_{zz}$ channel. That charge already contains the layer's electronic out-of-plane screening and is typically well below the bulk short-circuit Born charge; the placeholder 0.5 must be replaced by the computed value (see the "Which $Z^*_{zz}$" pitfall, §5). The alternative is to put out-of-plane screening into $\Pi^{\rm e}$ through a $z$-dipole channel, $\Pi^{\rm e}(z,z')\approx-\alpha_\parallel Q^2\delta(z)\delta(z')-\alpha_{zz}\,\delta'(z)\delta'(z')$ **[proposal]**, EPP's version of Royo–Stengel's 2×2 screening, and pair it with the short-circuit charge. Do not use both.

**(e) Radiative widths [derived].** Insert (a) with $\beta=-i\kappa$ inside the cone and $x=cQ/(\sqrt{\epsilon}\,\omega)$. The in-plane LO-like and TO-like modes have widths $\gamma_0\sqrt{1-x^2}$ and $\gamma_0/\sqrt{1-x^2}$ (primer). The ZO mode has
$$\gamma_{\rm ZO}=\gamma_{0,z}\,\frac{x^2}{\sqrt{1-x^2}},\qquad\hbar\gamma_{0,z}=\frac{C_z}{\hbar c\sqrt{\epsilon}},\qquad C_z=\frac{2\pi e^2\hbar^2Z^{*2}_{zz}}{S_{\rm uc}\,\mu}\ \ (\text{diatomic}),$$
with $Z^*_{zz}$ in units of $|e|$, as for the primer's $C$. Illustratively, the placeholder $Z^*_{zz}=0.5$ gives $\hbar\gamma_{0,z}\approx1.4$ μeV. The p-polarized poles of the full sheet $D$ are the 2D phonon polaritons; outside the cone they are the confined LO branch itself (Rivera *et al.*).

**(f) 2D Fröhlich vertex [proposal].** Carry E.416 to the sheet with $D^{\rm e}$. In E.416 the ion vertex $J^*_{\alpha\sigma}$ sits at the unprimed end of $D^{\rm e}_{\alpha\beta}$ and the electron current at the primed end, so with $\tilde\partial=(i\mathbf Q,\partial_z)$ acting on the ion height, the static limit reads, up to the overall phase and conjugation convention of E.448 and E p.98′,
$$g^{\rm 2D}_{mn\sigma}(\bk,\mathbf Q)\propto\frac{ie}{S_{\rm uc}}\sum_a\Big\langle m\bk+\mathbf Q\Big|\big[\mathbf Q\cdot\bj^{a*}_{\sigma\parallel}-i\,j^{a*}_{z\sigma}\partial_{z_a}\big]W^{\rm e}(\mathbf Q,0;z_a,z)\Big|n\bk\Big\rangle .$$
The relative sign of the two terms matters for in-plane/out-of-plane interference in layers without $\sigma_h$. With $W=v_Qe^{-Q|z-z_a|}$ both terms stay finite as $Q\to0$; the in-plane one is Sohier's 2D Fröhlich vertex, whose static limit the "Bridge to Giustino" box of [§B.10](../../sections/B10-2d.html) already derives. Check the out-of-plane static term against the out-of-plane dipole terms of Deng *et al.* (2021) and Poncé *et al.* (2023), which already contain it. For a $\sigma_h$-odd mode (ZO, A₂″) the potential is odd under $\sigma_h$, so intraband elements vanish in $\sigma_h$-even bands. The term survives in layers without $\sigma_h$ (Janus, buckled or chair: graphane, fluorographene) and for remote ZO scattering of carriers in an adjacent layer.

**(g) Multilayers [proposal].** With $W^{\rm e}(\mathbf Q,\omega;z_l,z_{l'})$ from a layer-resolved RPA (as in QEH), $\Omega^{\rm e}$ becomes a matrix in layer modes; its in-phase combination should reproduce the slope $\propto N$.

## 5. Pitfalls

- **Double counting with DFPT force constants.** 2D-cutoff DFPT already contains the $Q$-linear dipole terms and the analytic ZO contact shift. Subtract the 2D nonanalytic part (Sohier 2017; Royo–Stengel 2021) before adding $\Omega^{\rm e}$. Assign the contact term once, either to the short-range constants or to $\Omega^{\rm e}$ with a regularized form factor.
- **Which $Z^*_{zz}$.** Open-circuit (fixed $D_z$) and short-circuit (fixed $E_z$) charges differ by $\epsilon_{zz}$ (bulk relation). The vacuum kernel of §4(d) needs the open-circuit charge; a kernel that contains the layer's $\alpha_{zz}$ (a GW code's $W$ with $z$ local fields) needs the short-circuit one. Pairing the screened charge with an $\alpha_{zz}$- or $\epsilon_{zz}$-screened kernel counts the screening twice.
- **Double counting with $D$.** The Coulomb interaction is $D^{0l}$; never add $v$ on top. In a BSE response used as $\Pi^{\rm e}$ in the $z$-resolved Dyson equation, remove the $G_\parallel=0$ exchange for all $(G_z,G_z')$, since that whole block is the photon line $D^0(z,z')$. Keep the $G_\parallel\neq0$ exchange (in-plane local fields). In the strictly 2D $\delta(z)$ limit this reduces to dropping $\bG=0$ ([§B.5](../../sections/B05-photon-se.html)).
- **Gauge invariance.** E.310 (dia $=-$para(0)) holds for each $(\alpha,\beta)$ block, and atom pair by atom pair with E.300′/E.305′, only if the $\sigma$ sum runs over all $3s$ modes: E.308→E.309 uses completeness, $\sum_\sigma e_{a\mu\sigma}e^*_{b\nu\sigma}=\delta_{ab}\delta_{\mu\nu}$. It does not need all three photon components; dropping $z$ from both para and dia, as EPP does, is gauge consistent. Truncating modes in para but not in dia breaks it, e.g. omitting ZO/ZA from the $\sigma$ sum while $Z^*_{a,xz}\neq0$ in layers without $\sigma_h$. Use the subtracted form E.311, and E.281 per $(G_z,G_z')$ for electrons.
- **Sum rules.** $\sum_aZ^*_{a,\alpha\beta}=0$ for all $\alpha,\beta$; enforce it after DFPT. A violation gives a rigid translation a net dipole per cell (not a charge), so acoustic modes acquire a spurious dipole: a $\sqrt Q$ in-plane LA branch, $\omega^2\approx2\pi Q|\sum_aZ^*_a\cdot\hat{\mathbf e}|^2e^2/(S_{\rm uc}M)$, a gapped ZA mode with a spurious $-2\pi Q$ term, and a nonvanishing long-range acoustic vertex.
- **Conventions.** Gaussian $r_0=2\pi\alpha_{\rm 2D}$; from a supercell of height $L$, $r_0=L(\epsilon^{\rm sc}_\parallel-1)/2$ (Berkelbach *et al.* 2013); SI $r_0=\alpha_{\rm 2D}/2\varepsilon_0$; some write $\epsilon_{\rm env}(1+r_0'Q)$. For polar substrates use $\epsilon_{\rm env}$ at the phonon frequency, not the static value. A metallic substrate is not an $\epsilon_{\rm env}$ at all (§4(a)).
- **Regularization and convergence.** $\delta(0)$ and the ${\rm sgn}$ kink at $z_a=z_b$ need atomic form factors or many $G_z$; the integrable $1/\sqrt{1-x^2}$ at the light line needs finite $\eta$; $\mathbf Q$ sampling must resolve $Q^*=\epsilon_{\rm env}/r_0$.

## 7. First steps

1. **Derive and check (days).** Write out §4(a)–(e). Verify E.460 → E.472, the tracelessness of the **bare** $K$, the primer's 0.245 eV Å in the $c\to\infty$ limit and 42 μeV at finite $c$; add the ZO branch.
2. **Freestanding benchmark (1–2 weeks).** Cutoff DFPT (Quantum ESPRESSO, 2D cutoff) for $Z^*_\parallel$, $Z^*_{zz}$, $\epsilon$ and short-range IFCs. Confirm that the code returns the open-circuit 2D $Z^*_{zz}$, or convert the supercell short-circuit charge with $\epsilon_{zz}$, following Royo & Stengel (2021). Compare §4(d) for LO and ZO **only with freestanding cutoff DFPT** at small $Q$. Out-of-plane charges of hBN (and MoS₂) are small, so their ZO effect may be sub-meV and close to DFPT and $q$-interpolation noise. Therefore first reproduce Royo–Stengel's monolayer BN and SnS₂ ZO slopes, then Janus MoSSe, before any new system. Cross-check $r_0$ from your RPA code.
3. **Use your GW code's $W$ (2–4 weeks).** Use only the $G_\parallel=0$ block $W_{G_zG_z'}(\mathbf Q,\omega)$ of the truncated $W$ and project it onto atomic heights to get $K(z_a,z_b)$. That $W$ contains the layer's own $z$ local fields ($\alpha_{zz}$), so pair it with the short-circuit charge, not the open-circuit one (§5, "Which $Z^*_{zz}$"). Test on MoS₂ (E′ and A₂″) against the dipole and dipole-plus-quadrupole models.
4. **Environment (weeks).** Replace $D^0$ by the stack's dyadic Green's function with $\epsilon_{a,b}(\omega)$ (SiO₂; hBN as a substrate for other layers): hybrid sheet–substrate modes, remote Fröhlich scattering.
