Electron–phonon–photon primer · version 2 · pilot

A self-contained edition, tried on lattice dynamics

This pilot tries the format of the second edition on one section. It uses a single notation for electrons, phonons and photons, defines every symbol where it first appears, and can be read without the review by Giustino or the EPP manuscript at hand. Their symbols and equation numbers sit in the footnotes.

How to read it

  • An equation that introduces new symbols is followed by a where list.
  • Derivations go step by step, and every step says why it is taken.
  • Background boxes (violet) recap standard material. Skip them if you know it; the main text does not depend on them.
  • Footnote numbers open a note with the notation of the two sources and the matching equation numbers.
  • The Symbols button, bottom right, lists every symbol with its meaning and a link to its definition. Dotted symbols in the text open the same entry.

Version 1 of the primer stays available unchanged as the reference edition.
