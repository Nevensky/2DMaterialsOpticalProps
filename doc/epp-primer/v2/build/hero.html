Electron–phonon–photon primer · second edition

Electrons, phonons and photons, from lattice vibrations to the full electromagnetic field

This primer explains the first-principles theory of the electron–phonon interaction, following F. Giustino's review (Part A). It then explains how the electron–phonon–photon (EPP) formalism replaces the instantaneous Coulomb interaction of that theory by the full electromagnetic field, with retarded photons (Part B). A bridge in between compares the two. One notation serves electrons, phonons and photons throughout, every symbol is defined where it first appears, and the page can be read without the two sources at hand. Their symbols and equation numbers are given in the footnotes and in the source notes of each glossary.

How to read it

  • An equation that introduces new symbols is followed by a where list.
  • Derivations go step by step, and every step says why it is taken.
  • Background boxes (violet) recap standard material, such as second quantization, Green's functions or gauge freedom. Skip them if you know it. The main text quotes every result it uses from them.
  • Derivation boxes (collapsed, marked +) hold the algebra behind a result. The main text states every result and why it holds, so they can be skipped.
  • Footnote numbers open short notes: mostly the notation and equation numbers of the two sources, the SI form of key equations, and the corrections to the EPP manuscript.
  • The Symbols button, bottom right, lists every symbol with its meaning and a link to its definition. Dotted symbols in the text open the same entry.

Part A (A.1–A.9) follows the review. The bridge (X.1–X.2) shows where its Coulomb lines become photon lines. Part B (B.1–B.10) follows the EPP manuscript, with three corrections built in and footnoted. Version 1 of the primer keeps the sources' own notations and adds an outlook and an errata appendix. It stays available, unchanged, as the reference edition.
