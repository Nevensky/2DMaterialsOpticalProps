Electron–phonon–photon primer · version 2 · pilot

A self-contained edition, tried on lattice dynamics

This pilot tries the format of the second edition on one section. It uses a single notation for electrons, phonons and photons, defines every symbol where it first appears, and can be read without F. Giustino's review of electron–phonon interactions or the electron–phonon–photon (EPP) manuscript at hand (both are cited under Conventions). Their symbols and equation numbers sit in the footnotes and in the source notes of the glossary.

How to read it

  • An equation that introduces new symbols is followed by a where list.
  • Derivations go step by step, and every step says why it is taken.
  • Background boxes (violet) recap standard material. Skip them if you know it; the main text quotes every result it uses from them.
  • Derivation boxes (collapsed, marked +) hold the algebra behind a result. The main text states every result and why it holds, so they can be skipped.
  • Footnote numbers open short notes: mostly the notation and equation numbers of the two sources, plus a few side remarks.
  • The Symbols button, bottom right, lists every symbol with its meaning and a link to its definition. Dotted symbols in the text open the same entry.

Only the conventions and Section A.1 are part of this pilot; references to other sections point ahead to the full edition. Version 1 of the primer stays available unchanged as the reference edition.
