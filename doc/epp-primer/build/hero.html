Electrons, phonons and photons in crystals

A guided path from the first-principles electron–phonon theory reviewed by Giustino (2017) to the electron–phonon–photon (EPP) formalism. EPP puts the full electromagnetic field, transverse photons included, on the same footing as the lattice. Every section pairs the theory with figures you can drive.
