Notes on the sources

What turned up when every equation in this primer was checked against the two PDFs: inconsistencies in the EPP draft, a shorter list for Giustino's review, and the conventions that differ between them.

The EPP manuscript is a working draft. Its derivations are written out step by step, which is what made a line-by-line check possible. While building this primer we checked every displayed equation against the PDF and collected the inconsistencies below, so that they can be fixed in the next version. Each item was checked again against the PDF before it went into the table, and items that did not hold up were dropped. Page numbers refer to the PDF. Equation numbers are the manuscript's, written E.n as elsewhere on this page.

Most items are typos, notation or unit bookkeeping. The items marked physics mostly correct an intermediate statement or its justification. None of the items overturns the manuscript's end results: the Dyson equation for the total propagator (E.379–E.380), the electrostatic phonon self-energy (E.452–E.454) and the 2D results (E.471–E.472). Three items go beyond typos: they change equations, or how a displayed result should be read. They are now corrected with replacement equations in §Z.3, and their rows say so and give the primed tags. The basis phase missing from E.121–E.131 (item 14) does not matter as $q\to0$ for optical modes, but does for acoustic modes, for $\bG\neq0$ and at finite $q$ in cells with more than one atom. E.410 is the $q\to0$, finite-$c$, electron-free point of the upper phonon polariton, not the LO–TO splitting (item 47); it differs from the electrostatic E.454 by electronic screening, which accounts for 56 against 36 meV in GaAs, and by the order of limits, which moves only the TO modes. The charge $e$ lost in the continuity equation on p. 94 is the "missing $e^2$" the author notes on p. 96: the electrostatic Fröhlich vertices on pp. 95 and 98 gain a factor $e$ and then reduce to Verdi–Giustino (item 54). The same slip leaves the 2D bubble on p. 101 without $e^2$ (item 60). Several smaller items (3, 12, 15, 28, 45, 48, 51–53) are settled by the same corrections.

Conventions to keep in mind when reading both

  • Units. Giustino is SI throughout ($e^2/4\pi\varepsilon_0=14.40$ eV Å). EPP §3 is SI, and §4 onward is Gaussian. Convert with $e^2/4\pi\varepsilon_0\leftrightarrow e^2$ and $1/\varepsilon_0\leftrightarrow4\pi$. Every displayed equation in this primer says which system it uses (§X.2, §B.2).
  • Born charges. Giustino's $\bZ^*_\kappa$ is dimensionless. EPP's $Z^*$ carries the charge, $Z^*_{\rm EPP}=|e|\,Z^*_{\rm G}$, which hides one factor $e$ whenever the two papers' vertices are compared (§B.1, §X.2).
  • Number versus charge density. EPP's $\rho_{\rm e}=\Psi^\dagger\Psi$ counts electrons, while $\rho_{\rm ion}=-\nabla\cdot\bP$ and $\bj_{\rm e}$ carry charge. A factor $e$ belongs wherever $\rho_{\rm e}$ meets a charge: Gauss's law, the Coulomb energy, continuity with $\bj$ (§B.4, §B.8, §Z.3).
  • Name clashes. $D$ is Giustino's nuclear density correlator, displacement correlator and phonon propagator (G Eqs. 103, 109, 132), next to his dynamical matrix $D^{\rm dm}$; in EPP it is the photon propagator. $\Pi$ is Giustino's phonon self-energy and EPP's photon self-energy $\Pi_{jj}$. $\Omega$ is the unit-cell volume in Giustino; in EPP it is the crystal volume (the cell is $\Omega_{\rm uc}$), the force-constant matrix $\Omega^2$ of §1 and the phonon self-energy $\Omega^{\rm e}$. $\sigma$ is EPP's phonon branch, photon polarization (E.219) and $l/t$ label (E.324), and the conductivity elsewhere. This primer writes Giustino's phonon propagator $\mathcal D$ and his phonon self-energy $\Pi^{\rm ph}$ (§X.2).
  • Two meanings of "retardation". Giustino, following Bardeen and Pines, means frequency-dependent electronic screening, $\epsilon(\bq,\omega)$ in place of $\epsilon(\bq,0)$ (G pp. 4, 12, 18). EPP means the finite speed of light, and its $c\to\infty$ limit is the instantaneous, non-retarded one (§X.1).
  • Time-ordered versus retarded. Both papers mostly write $T=0$ time-ordered propagators with $+i\eta$, which agree with retarded functions only for $\omega\gt0$. Giustino's finite-temperature Fan–Migdal form (Eq. 158) is retarded, so his signs of $\Gamma_{n\bk}$ for electrons and holes hold for the time-ordered Eq. 157 only. The figures in this primer use retarded forms, $\omega^2-\omega_0^2+i\gamma\omega$ (§A.7, §A.8, §B.5).
  • Normalizations. Giustino's $g$ (G Eq. 38) is $N_p$-independent, with $N_p^{-1/2}$ in the Hamiltonian; EPP puts $N^{-1/2}$ inside $g$. Giustino's phonon $\gamma$ is a half width ($\tau^{\rm ph}=(2\gamma)^{-1}$, p. 23), and his $N_F$ is not declared per spin; the heat capacity (Eq. 192) and the Eliashberg formulas (Eqs. 205–210) imply per spin, the Saitta formula with Eq. 145 both spins (Giustino item 11; §A.2, §A.6).
