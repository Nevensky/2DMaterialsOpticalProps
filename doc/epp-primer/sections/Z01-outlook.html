What EPP adds, and what is still open

The two formalisms side by side in eight statements, a table of where the electromagnetic extension changes the numbers, and the pieces the EPP manuscript has not yet worked out.

Eight statements to take away

  1. Same lattice, same normal modes. Both start from harmonic force constants, the dynamical matrix and phonon ladder operators (§A.1, §B.1). The difference is which force constants are allowed. EPP must use only the short-range (analytic) part, because it regenerates the long-range dipole–dipole part itself.
  2. Giustino folds the Coulomb physics into numbers. EPP keeps it as a field. In Part A, $\varepsilon_{n\bk}$, $\omega_{\bq\nu}$ and the screened vertex $g_{mn\nu}(\bk,\bq)$ already contain all Coulomb effects (§A.2, §A.3). In Part B the Lagrangian has no electron–phonon term at all. Electrons and ions talk only through the vector potential (§B.2).
  3. Coulomb is the longitudinal photon. In the temporal gauge, the energy of the longitudinal field becomes, via Gauss's law, the full instantaneous Coulomb interaction of electrons and ionic bound charges (§B.4). Charge conservation then rewrites every density vertex as a current vertex. It also turns $v$ into $D^{0l}$, so the free propagator is the vacuum Maxwell propagator $D^0=D^{0l}+D^{0t}$ (§B.6).
  4. One self-energy for all matter. Giustino's polarization $P=\delta\langle\hat n\rangle/\delta V_{\rm tot}$ (G.89), the response of the total electron-plus-nuclear charge, becomes EPP's current–current self-energy $\Pi_{jj}=\Pi^{\rm e}+\Pi^{\rm ion}$. It is gauge invariant only when the diamagnetic terms are kept, $\Pi=\Pi^{\rm para}(\omega)-\Pi^{\rm para}(0)$ (§B.5). The ionic part is exact in the harmonic approximation.
  5. $W$ is a projection of $D$. Giustino's $W=W_{\rm e}+W_{\rm e}\mathcal D W_{\rm e}$ (§A.5) is the longitudinal–longitudinal sector of EPP's $D=D^0+D^0\Pi_{jj}D$, and $W^{\rm e}=D^{\rm e}_{11}$ (§B.9).
  6. The order of limits matters. At $q=0$ with finite $c$, the mode that shifts is the upper phonon polariton, with an unscreened oscillator strength (E.410). The familiar electrostatic LO–TO splitting appears only when $c\to\infty$ is taken first (E.454), and it then coincides with the Gonze–Lee nonanalytic term (§B.7, §B.9).
  7. The Fröhlich vertex becomes dynamical. Photon exchange between an electronic and an ionic current gives a frequency-dependent vertex. For $c\to\infty$ it reduces to the Verdi–Giustino vertex with $\epsilon^\infty\to\hat\epsilon^{\rm e}(\bq,\omega)$. The phonon self-energy keeps Giustino's structure of one bare and one screened vertex (§B.8, §A.6).
  8. In 2D there is no LO–TO splitting at Γ. With the strictly two-dimensional $W^{\rm e,2D}=v_Q/\epsilon^{\rm e}_{2D}$ the LO shift grows linearly from zero. The screening length $r_0$ that sets its saturation is the same one that controls Rytova–Keldysh exciton screening (§B.10).

For RPA, GW and BSE practitioners

Most objects in Part B are already familiar from these methods, written in vector form. The electronic photon self-energy $\Pi^{\rm e}$ is the Adler–Wiser response with current vertices. Its longitudinal projection, $\Pi_{\rho\rho}=-\frac{c}{\omega^2}\,\bq\cdot\Pi_{jj}\cdot\bq$, is the familiar density response $\chi^0$. The screened interaction $W^{\rm e}$ that dresses the phonons in §B.9 and §B.10 is the RPA $W$ without local fields. What is new is the transverse sector. It matters only inside the light cone, $q\lesssim q_c=\sqrt{\epsilon_\infty}\,\omega/c$, which is $10^{-5}$–$10^{-4}$ Å$^{-1}$ for vibrational frequencies and about $10^{-3}$ Å$^{-1}$ for a 2 eV exciton (§X.1).

Where the electromagnetic extension changes the numbers

The two kinds of retardation of §X.1 sort the observables. A finite speed of light acts only inside the light cone just quoted. Frequency-dependent electronic screening acts at any $q$ wherever $\hat\epsilon^{\rm e}(\bq,\omega)$ has structure near the phonon frequencies; Giustino's theory contains it, and EPP keeps it in the vertex instead of setting $g(\omega)\simeq g(0)$ (§B.8). Outside these two windows Part A already gives the answer.

What the manuscript does not yet contain

  • Short-range coupling. The EPP Lagrangian freezes the crystal potential at equilibrium, so deformation-potential coupling (acoustic phonons, non-polar optical phonons) is absent. Only the polar, long-range channel is extended.
  • Electron self-energies. EPP dresses photons and phonons but derives no electron self-energy from combined photon and phonon exchange. Giustino's Fan–Migdal and Debye–Waller terms (§A.7) have no EPP counterpart yet. Neither do the temperature-dependent gaps and lifetimes built on them (§A.8).
  • Exchange–correlation, local fields and excitons. The electronic response is RPA without crystal local-field effects (no $\bG,\bG'$ dependence after E.380) and without $f^{xc}$; DFPT screening (§A.3) is static but includes both. Nor is there an electron–hole ladder: $\Pi^{\rm e}$ is the independent-particle bubble, so the theory has no excitons. Replacing $\Pi^{\rm e}$ by the BSE current–current response, with its long-range ($\bG=0$) exchange term removed because that term is the longitudinal photon line of $D$ itself, would give exciton polaritons; exciton–phonon coupling would need phonon lines inside the BSE kernel. Neither is in the manuscript (§B.5).
  • Phonon-assisted optics. Light is now an internal field. The indirect absorption that Giustino treats with a classical field (§A.9) has not been rederived inside EPP.
  • Out-of-plane dipoles in 2D. The 2D results keep only in-plane modes and use a strictly 2D screening model. The ZO modes with $Z^*_{zz}$, and the full sheet propagator with retardation, are left for later.
  • Polaritons beyond the zone centre. The transverse sector of E.380 with $\Pi^{\rm ion}$ contains the full phonon-polariton dispersion, but the manuscript works out only its $q=0$ end point (E.410); the isotropic branches are derived on this page (§B.6, §B.7). Anisotropic crystals, where $\Pi_{jj}$ mixes the L and T sectors off the principal axes, are not treated, so the hyperbolic phonon polaritons of hBN are outside the manuscript as written.
